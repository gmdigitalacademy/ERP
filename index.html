📖 User Manual

🏠 Dashboard

Snapshot of today's attendance, and four premium progress-ring donuts: Overall (all included years, with a toggle to include/exclude 1st Year), Attendance by Year (pick any combination of years), Attendance by Department (pick any combination of departments), and Attendance by Subject (today's subjects, from the timetable). Tap ⚙️ Include/Exclude to control which departments/years count toward the totals. Every section here can be collapsed using the ▾ button in its top-right corner.

🎓 Students

Add, edit, search, filter and sort students. Sort by S.No, Name, Register No, CGPA, Arrears, Attendance %, Category, Gender, Father's Name or Phone — with an Asc/Desc toggle. In the Add/Edit form, Gender is asked first — Category (Day Scholar / College Hostel / Private Hostel) only unlocks after that, and only offers the Boys or Girls version matching the Gender picked. Tap ⚠️ Missing Data to see incomplete records and edit them directly. Tap 🗓️ Attendance to mark a period; tapping a student's name there opens their full profile.

🧑‍🏫 Faculties

Manage faculty records, subjects handled (auto-fetched from the Subjects list), and mark daily faculty attendance/OD.

📅 Timetable

Build each department/year's weekly timetable — subject code, name and faculty auto-fill from what's already on file. Configure period timings, short break and lunch generally, per year, or per weekday (e.g. a different Friday lunch slot), with a custom period order.

📊 HOD / Principal Report

Generate a shareable attendance report for any combination of years and periods — total/present/absent/OD breakdown, 3+ day continuous-leave list and below-threshold attendance list, all with user-defined thresholds.

📚 Subjects & Outcome-Based Education

Manage the subject list per semester. Each subject's 📘 button opens Syllabus (topics, hours and pedagogy), Course Outcomes, and a CO–PO/PSO attainment matrix (0–3 per cell) with an auto-computed overall Attainment Value. PEO/PO/PSO are shared department-wide via "🎯 Dept PEO/PO/PSO".

⚙️ Settings

Set the mandatory institution details (College Name, Department Name, Academic Year) once — a banner on the Dashboard will remind you until they're filled in. Optionally show the College/Department name at the top of every screen. Adjust the app-wide font size with A−/A+ (also available as quick buttons in the header), check the current app version, browse the version history, or open this manual again.

🗄️ Archive Old Attendance
i
Attendance keeps growing every day it's marked, and all of it is kept in the same record that syncs to the cloud on every edit — so after a few years this can make the app feel slower. Archiving moves attendance marked before a date you choose out of that live record and into a downloaded backup file, freeing it up. It's entirely optional and only affects attendance — students, faculty, marks, timetable etc. are never touched. Archived dates will no longer appear in reports, the Dashboard or OD Report — keep the downloaded file safe if you may ever need to look them up again.
